About us

About Zero Cube

A Section 8 not-for-profit company advancing Net Zero, SROI and Quality Education across India.

In one line

Zero Cube is a not-for-profit company that works for Net Zero, SROI and quality education — for the people, by the people.

Our Belief

One family, one planet, one future

We humans are the most intelligent species on this earth. With that gift comes a duty: to create a happy, equal world — not only for people, but for every living being — with the help of nature. Nature alone helps us grow and evolve, and it is nature that will make our future generations happy.

Our tradition says it simply: Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam — the world is one family. The rivers, forests, soils and creatures we share this earth with belong to that family, and so do the children not yet born. What we use today, we hold in trust for them.

Net Zero

is not only a compliance pledge. It is a way of living happily and healthily in harmony with nature — cleaner air, active days, wholesome food, less waste.

SROI

makes good measurable and trusted, so every rupee given for society shows the change it created.

Quality Education

is an equal right of every child — whatever their gender, caste, religion, income, location or ability.

NPOs

are society's changemakers. We help their work be seen, understood and supported.

This is not a mission for a few experts. It is for the people, by the people. Join us — as a student, a teacher, a company, an NPO, a village, or simply as someone who cares.

Who we are

For the people, by the people

Zero Cube Climate & Social Impact Foundation was incorporated on 28 September 2026 as a not-for-profit company under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013, with its registered office in Pune. We are a framework designer and certifier for social impact and Net Zero, and a delivery engine for quality education and NetZero villages funded by CSR and philanthropy.

Mission

To design and steward Net Zero, ESG and SROI frameworks, certification programmes and CSR delivery models that help organisations meet rising expectations while funding lasting social and climate impact at community scale.

Vision

To become a recognised framework designer — building certification programmes organisations can trust, and using CSR as a delivery engine for NetZero villages, a green economy that creates dignified work, and quality education with an understanding of moral responsibility towards the climate.

Three pillars

What we stand for

Net Zero

Scope 3 value-chain assessments for corporates, footprinting and training for MSME vendors, certification under our criteria — and Net Zero as a happier, healthier way to live.

Learn more →

SROI — Social Return on Investment

We measure the social, environmental and economic value created for every ₹1 invested, following the Principles of Social Value.

Learn more →

Quality Education

Education is a fundamental right. Every child deserves an equal opportunity to learn — whatever their gender, caste, religion, income, location or ability — with moral responsibility towards the climate.

Objects

Our objects in brief

  • Design and steward ESG and Net Zero frameworks, criteria and certification programmes for villages, clubs, schools, MSMEs, events and organisations
  • Measure and report social and environmental value: SROI studies, impact assessments and dMRV
  • Climate and sustainability education and awareness: webinars, courses, school, college, teacher and youth programmes
  • Use CSR and philanthropic funds for NetZero villages, green rural livelihoods and quality education
  • Build the capacity of NGOs, trusts, panchayats and small enterprises to measure, report and raise funds for impact

Summary only; the Memorandum of Association is the governing text.

Governance

Not-for-profit by design

Section 8 company

Limited by guarantee, no share capital. Income and property are applied only to our objects.

No dividend

No profit is distributed to members or directors.

Spending follows income

No commitment is made before funds are received; directors give no personal guarantees or loans.

Our motto

For the people, by the people

Zero Cube is shaped by the people it serves. Members, volunteers and communities do not just benefit from our work — they help decide and deliver it.

Open company membership

Anyone aged 18+ resident in India who supports our objects can earn company membership through engagement, endorsement and Board admission — it is never sold.

Student Crew & volunteers

Students and volunteers run webinars, collect field data, write and lead campus chapters.

Village voices

Gram Panchayats and village committees lead their own Net Zero journey, and village representatives have a pathway into company membership.

Transparent reporting

Certificates, project reports and an annual transparency report are published for everyone to see.

One Vision, All Members

Students, individuals, startups and MSMEs, corporates, institutions, NPOs, villages and experts each bring something different — time, funding, classrooms, solutions, projects or know-how — to one shared vision.
