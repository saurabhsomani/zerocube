Transparency

Where your membership fee goes

As a Section 8 not-for-profit company, every rupee we receive is applied only to our objects. No dividend is ever paid, and our directors draw no pay from Zero Cube.

First-year budget estimate

How each ₹100 of spending is planned

₹64.5 Programmes — Net Zero work, SROI, education and awareness
₹22.7 Management & compliance — audit, filings, governance
₹12.7 Member services & fundraising

Base case of our six-month self-funded budget (Oct 2026 – Mar 2027), as a share of allocated spending. An estimate, not an audited result; percentages are rounded.

What your fee helps build

Programmes

Net Zero work, education and school programmes.

Free awareness

Public webinars open to everyone.

Certification & SROI tools

Criteria, frameworks and the value bank.

Impact Portal & dMRV

Digital evidence and published reports.

Member services

Sessions, courses, bulletin and support.

Compliance & audit

Statutory audit, filings and governance.

Spending follows income

We commit money only after it is received. Directors give no loans or guarantees.

Villages never pay

Village work is funded by sponsors and CSR, never by the village.

Members build the ecosystem

Fees fund the shared frameworks, tools and free awareness that every member benefits from.

Annual transparency report

Each year we will publish how income was received and applied, alongside our audited accounts.
