Our story

Why we began

Before Zero Cube was born, our founders spent years working hand in hand with local, regional, national and international organisations like Rotary and the Environmental Club of India, with schools and colleges, with local bodies like Gram Panchayats, and with grassroots groups. Together they planted trees, ran training programmes, hosted seminars, and brought leaders together at the Conference on Circular Economy and the Seminar on Net Zero.

That groundwork brought real, measurable change: 34 Net Zero certificates (1 Gram Panchayat and 33 clubs) from our founders’ earlier field work, before Zero Cube was formed.

More importantly, it taught us a simple truth: people want to act, but they need clear rules, honest proof and a trusted guide to walk the path with them.

That is why our founders set up Zero Cube: to unite climate change mitigation with a strong social movement.

Founders' track record: 34 Net Zero certificates (1 Gram Panchayat, 33 clubs) issued in 2025-26 in association with Rotary District 3131's Environment Team, before Zero Cube was incorporated on 28 September 2026. They are not Zero Cube certificates, and no current partnership or MoU with Rotary is implied.
